A_Light
=======

.. action:: A_Light
	:class: Weapon

	Slightly increases the light level. Typically used with gun flashes.

	:param intensity: Amount of adjustment to be made to the light level from -20 to 20. Negative values decrease the light level.

.. action:: A_Light0
	:class: Weapon

	Alias for :expr:`A_Light(0)`.

	:class: Weapon

	Alias for :expr:`A_Light(1)`.

	:class: Weapon

	Alias for :expr:`A_Light(2)`.
