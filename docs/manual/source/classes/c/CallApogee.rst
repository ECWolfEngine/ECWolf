.. decorate-actor-header:: CallApogee

Overview
--------

Secret sign that was part of a contest from Apogee. The presence of level editors called the contest off and the sprite was eventually replaced with a :class:`pile of gibs <Bones3>` (although thicker in appearance from that actor). The decoration is commonly used to get a new solid decoration in mods.
